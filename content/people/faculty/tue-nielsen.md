Tue Kjærgaard Nielsen is a Tenure Track Assistant Professor at the Department of Plant and Environmental Sciences, University of Copenhagen. He is a molecular microbiologist specializing in microbial evolution and the role of mobile genetic elements.

### Research Focus
*   **PFAS Biodegradation:** Investigating microbial pathways for the degradation of PFAS and fluoride toxicity.
*   **Genomics & Bioinformatics:** Utilizing 3rd generation sequencing (Nanopore) for genetic context analysis and antibiotic resistance prediction.
*   **Mobile Genetic Elements:** Studying the impact of MGEs and bacteriophages on microbial evolution.

### Background
Tue earned his PhD in Environmental Science from Aarhus University and has extensive experience in bioinformatic analysis of exotic DNA modifications and microbial ecology.
