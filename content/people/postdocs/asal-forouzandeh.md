Asal Forouzandeh is a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Microbial Ecology and Biotechnology section at the Department of Plant and Environmental Sciences (PLEN). Her research focuses on understanding and engineering microbial systems for biotechnological applications.

### Research Interests
*   **Microbial Ecology:** Studying the interactions and dynamics of microbial communities in various environments.
*   **Biotechnology:** Developing microbial-based solutions for environmental and industrial challenges.
*   **Microbial Systems:** Analyzing the functional and genetic basis of microbial metabolism.
