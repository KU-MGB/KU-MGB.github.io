Shaban Ahmad is a Postdoctoral Scientist at the University of Copenhagen. His work focuses on the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Biology, specifically in the areas of enzyme mining and translational computational pharmacology.

### Research Interests
*   **AI for Biodegradation:** Developing AI-driven bioinformatics pipelines for mining novel enzymes capable of PFAS degradation.
*   **Computational Pharmacology:** Applying deep learning (CNNs, RNNs, Transformers) to drug design and de novo ligand discovery.
*   **Precision Oncology:** Researching variant calling and pathogenicity prediction through advanced AI models.

### Background
Shaban holds a PhD in AI in Biology and an MSc in Bioinformatics from Jamia Millia Islamia. He has previously served as a Visiting Researcher at the University of Jyväskylä.
