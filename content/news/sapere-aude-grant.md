---
title: "Tue Kjærgaard Nielsen awarded Sapere Aude DFF Starting Grant"
date: "2025-05-15"
category: "Award"
description: "DFF awards DKK 6.2 million for PFAS degradation research."
---
Tue Kjærgaard Nielsen has received a prestigious **Sapere Aude: DFF Starting Grant** for the project \"Solving Microbial Degradation of PFAS\". This grant will allow the MGB Lab to establish a world-class research group dedicated to identifying the biological mechanisms for breaking down forever chemicals.
