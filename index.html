Join Us

We are always looking for motivated students and postdocs to help uncover enzymes that break down persistent pollutants.

BSc / MSc Projects

We offer highly supervised projects in bioinformatics, metagenomics, and enzyme assays.

PhD / Postdocs

Funded positions will be announced via KU portals, but we also welcome candidates bringing their own fellowships.

Research Areas

PFAS defluorination, metagenomics, enzyme engineering, and environmental biotechnology — see the Research and Projects sections for what we're actively working on.

What you'll gain

  • Hands-on training across the full pipeline — sequencing, bioinformatics, structural modelling, and wet-lab assays
  • Direct supervision and a genuinely collaborative lab environment
  • Co-authorship on publications where your contribution warrants it

How it works

  1. 1 Submit the form with your CV and a short statement of interest.
  2. 2 We review your background against current openings and ongoing projects.
  3. 3 Shortlisted candidates are invited for an informal interview.
